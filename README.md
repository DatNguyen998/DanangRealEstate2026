---

### 🗂️ Preliminary 2026 Observations
As I pull together data from recent market reports to build my datasets, I am documenting a few key trends shaping the city this year:
*   **Apartment Pricing:** As of early 2026, the average primary price for apartments has reached approximately 83 million VND/m2. 
*   **Segment Shifts:** The land plot segment is gradually warming up and seeing slight price increases. Meanwhile, the resort and condotel segments remain relatively quiet.
*   **Infrastructure Impact:** Major regional developments are acting as significant drivers for new housing demand and investment. Key projects driving this shift include the Lien Chieu container terminal and the newly approved free trade zone.
